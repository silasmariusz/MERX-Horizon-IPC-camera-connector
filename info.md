# MERX Horizon IPC Camera

Home Assistant integration for MERX Horizon IP Cameras.

## Features
- **Live Stream:** Main and Sub streams via RTSP.
- **PTZ Control:** Pan, Tilt, Zoom support directly from Home Assistant.
- **Events:** Motion, Face, Human, Vehicle, LPR, Fire & Smoke, and more.
- **Media Source:** Browse and playback recordings from the camera's SD card.
